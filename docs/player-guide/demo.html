從營火學會經營與防守

  1. 一開始就站在營火旁幾步。鬼魂會陪走、回頭等你；每換一個任務就立刻說出要做什麼，走動中也會說，離目標遠時說「跟我來」，停太久會再提醒。引導結束後，鬼魂會持續喊出當下目標：建造、採集龍晶、撐過夜襲、封門。靠近目標時用手勢指引，圖示縮到手邊，避開騎士、按鈕與互動資訊；可略過或重新顯示。
  2. 主遊戲選「開始旅程」，Demo 選「開始森林冒險」，兩者都直接引導。先點火、招募、製造錘子，再到附近樹木投入一晶委託採集。靠近工匠收晶後，才開放弓與防線。
  3. 右牆完工並配置弓兵後，就開始首夜 20 秒預警；弓兵走到城牆前不會入夜。配置守軍免費，人員會走過去；略過提示不會跳過實際採集條件。
  4. 首夜後開放完整森林：龍晶商人、遺跡解謎取得部件、戰馬與拓荒。西側安靜補給，東側有守獸，第二夜左右先後來襲。兩夜後帶工匠封印龍門、召喚並擊敗森林巨龍。到營火旁領取龍核，再到營火西側投入 20 晶讓工匠建好火箭，Demo 即完成並回到主選單；主遊戲則搭火箭繼續七星旅程。
  5. 新主旅程與 Demo 普攻不耗體力，但須先拔劍。衝刺耗盡仍能一般跑步，移動中恢復至30%可再衝刺；舊紀錄保留原恢復規則。
  6. 暫停後右側可重試準備階段；戰敗先等黑幕結束。收入後與首夜後各有準備點，完整還原資源、工作和世界，不保留後來收益。兩夜教學結束後準備點退役；舊 Demo 兩夜紀錄仍可重試。
  7. 電腦：A/D移動、同方向雙按並按住衝刺、J或滑鼠左鍵攻擊、E互動、Q丟晶、Esc暫停。觸控：拖曳移動、劍鈕攻擊、靠近後點投入按鈕。
  8. v0.0.60 起 Demo 是完整森林冒險：主遊戲與 Demo 的包、名稱和存檔分開，共用玩法由主線同步；試玩站根目錄是 Demo（不含其餘六星關卡），main/ 路徑是主遊戲網頁版。
  9. 本版更新：箭塔一至三級可派1／2／3名弓兵，配置介面可查看總數、未指派與到位數；未指派獵人白天出城巡獵、晚上進城。馬廄移至西側獨立空地，戰馬跑動減少跳格。讀檔後可點選單內的播放圖示返回畫面。
  10. 鬼魂改為更小的青藍電漿夥伴，保留帶路與招手；移動時本體穩定發光，避開按鈕和提示。 新旅程森林近郊左右各有三隻獵物，遠處林區也有少量鹿、野兔和野豬。未指派獵人白天出城狩獵，天黑前回城，每次成功仍得2晶；保留森林才能在日出恢復獵物。舊存檔保留原數量。
  11. 可狩獵生物現在有八種：鹿、野兔、野豬、狐狸、獾、山羊、雉雞、陸龜，各有不同輪廓與步態。近郊和遠林分散出現，數量不再加密；獵人照原規則狩獵，每次仍得2晶。舊紀錄保留原獵物數量與生死狀態。
  12. 以鹿的細緻像素風格統一其餘七種動物：野兔、野豬、狐狸、獾、山羊、雉雞與陸龜都有完整八格動作，補上毛皮、羽毛及甲殼層次。走動、停步與轉身保持身形和腳底位置一致。原有狩獵收益、數量與存檔維持原規則。

Learn to build and defend your refuge

  1. You start a few steps from the campfire. The spirit travels with you and waits; it names each new task at once, even while you move, says "This way" when the target is far, and reminds you if you stop too long. After the guided opening it keeps calling the goal of the moment: build, gather crystals, survive the night raid, seal the gates. Beside the target it gestures; small symbols sit by its hand, clear of the knight, controls and interaction information. Hide or restore guidance at any time.
  2. Choose Begin Journey in the main game, or Begin the Forest Adventure in the demo; both start with guidance. Light the fire, recruit, make a hammer, then pay one crystal at a nearby tree. Collect from the worker to unlock bows and defences.
  3. Once the right wall is built and an archer is assigned, the 20-second first-night warning begins; night will not fall until that archer reaches the wall. Assignments are free and require travel. Hiding hints does not bypass the actual harvest.
  4. After night one the whole forest opens: the crystal merchant, ruin puzzles that award modules, the warhorse and new outposts. Quiet supplies lie west and a guarded nest lies east; night two attacks both sides in sequence. Then escort engineers to seal the gates, summon the forest dragon and defeat it. Claim its core beside the campfire and invest 20 crystals so engineers build the rocket west of camp; the finished rocket completes the demo and returns to the title screen. The main game flies on to the other worlds.
  5. Ordinary attacks are free in new main journeys and the demo, but require your sword. Exhaustion becomes normal running; recover while moving and sprint again at 30%. Older saves retain their recovery rules.
  6. Pause to retry preparation on the right; after defeat, wait for the blackout. Checkpoints follow first harvest income and the first night. Resources, work and the world restore fully without later gains. Preparation retries retire after the two-night introduction; older two-night demo saves can still retry.
  7. Keyboard: A/D move; double-tap and hold a direction to sprint; J or left click attacks; E interacts; Q drops a crystal; Esc pauses. Touch: drag to move, use the sword button and the nearby payment button.
  8. From v0.0.60 the demo is the whole forest adventure. Main-game and demo packages, names and saves stay separate; gameplay updates come from the same main branch. The trial site root runs the demo, without the six later worlds; the main/ path runs the main game's web build.
  9. Update: tier 1/2/3 towers hold 1/2/3 archers. Guard orders show total, free and arrived troops. Free hunters patrol outside by day and return before night. The stable has its own western clearing, horse animation skips fewer poses, and the pause menu has a play icon to return after loading.
  10. The spirit is now a smaller cyan plasma companion. It still leads and gestures, keeps a steady core while moving, and stays clear of controls and hints. New forest journeys have three prey animals beyond each side of camp, plus small groups of deer, hares and boars in distant woodlands. Unassigned hunters hunt by day, return before night, and still earn 2 crystals per catch. Standing woodland renews prey at dawn. Older saves keep their population.
  11. There are now eight huntable species: deer, hare, boar, fox, badger, goat, pheasant and tortoise, each with its own silhouette and gait. They are spread across nearby and distant woodland without increasing population density. Hunters follow the same rules and still earn 2 crystals per catch. Existing saves keep their prey counts and survival state.
  12. The seven other huntable animals now match the detailed pixel-art style of the deer. Hare, boar, fox, badger, goat, pheasant and tortoise each have eight complete animation poses with detailed fur, feathers or shell shading. Movement, stopping and turning use a consistent body scale and ground anchor. Hunting rewards, population and saved progress follow the existing rules.
