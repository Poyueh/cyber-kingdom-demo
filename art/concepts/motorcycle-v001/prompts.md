## Riders

Draw ONLY THE COMPLETE RIDER, no motorcycle, no wheels, no scenery. 4 columns x 2 rows = EIGHT full-body poses on real transparent background, equal spaced identical cells. All face exactly RIGHT strict flat side profile. SAME slim body proportions and scale, NOT broad/stocky, no shoulder growth between frames. Hips remain in exactly same cell position, legs bent forward/down, boots on imaginary low foot pegs. Left hand always reaches to imaginary handlebar to right of belly, torso natural strong lean forward. Each full rider about 58 game-pixels tall if reduced to native sprite scale, crisp coherent pixel blocks as reference. Frames reading order: 1 balanced seated idle left hand on handlebar right hand low holding sword angled down and forward; 2 forward leaned acceleration right hand still has sword low; 3 attack begins pulling sword back behind shoulder; 4 sword raised over right shoulder ready to cut, torso twists minimally; 5 strong forward slash sword horizontally extended towards right in front of rider at chest height; 6 follow-through sword low forwards returns towards idle; 7 hit reaction shoulders lean slightly back hips planted no dismount; 8 resting both hands steady on bars without a weapon. Design SAME blacksteel knight in all cells, matching helmet, shoulder plates, waist width, boot shapes. Complete figures fully in each cell, all visible extremities including swords, no overlaps/cropped blades. No text, no cell borders, no antialias blur, no horses, no other characters. Image canvas 1536 wide by 1024 high if possible, each cell ample padding and identical anchor at hips. True transparent alpha.
